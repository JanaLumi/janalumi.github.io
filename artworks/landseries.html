Land Series

This series of paintings is a telling of the Earth, the land, all who shift with the land.

It is the interdependence of bodies that shapes life. All in existence is in relationship and move in relation to each other. Relational expression requires a call and response.

Back and forth, they are interdependent with each other. It is not so much causal, as it is relational. An occurrence doesn't happen because of something else, but rather they happen in reciprocity.

There is always something that came before and there is always something that comes after. There are cycles. The cycles may not be noticed initally, yet they persist. The orbits might be small or they might be large. There might be greater distance in time between each occurrence. The spin is ever present.

The first painting is the smallest. It's the forms, shapes and colours of the land. The water that travels through it; the swathes of grasses, soil, waters that cover the planet; and the depths that draw us in. The second is the moon, the sun, the darkness and the water. All is in relationship. The third is a cascade: a waterfall showering, crashing down from heights to the lows.

From the water comes the four paintings, bringing the plants, roses, trees, grasses, leaves, and all who rise up from the Earth. The birds live among the trees, the flowers and the plants, as do insects and all who depend on our shared cohabitation.
