Land Series

This series of paintings is a telling a story of the Earth and the land and the things that grow and the way the land moves and shapes.

It's relationship to bodies around it, like the moon and the sun, and elements such as water and the growth of plants and trees. All of these are in relationship and they move in relation to each other. Their expression too is relational. It's always a call and a response.

Back and forth, they are interdependent with each other. It is not so much as it is causal, but as it is relational. One thing doesn't happen because of something else, but rather they happen in response to each other.

There is always something that came before and there is always something that comes after. There is a cycle. We may not initially notice the cycles, but they are always there. The orbits might be small or they might be large. There might be greater distance in time between each occurrence.

The first painting is the smallest. It's the forms and shapes and colors of the land. It's the water that travels through it and it is the swathe of grasses, soil, waters and depths that cover the planet. The second is the moon and the sun and the darkness and the water. Everything in relationship with each other. The third one is a cascade. A waterfall showering and crashing down from heights to the lows.

From the water comes the four painting, bringing the plants, the roses, the trees, the grasses, the leaves, and all that rise up from the Earth. The birds live from the trees and the flowers and the plants, as do the insects and all the other lives that depend on our shared cohabitation.
